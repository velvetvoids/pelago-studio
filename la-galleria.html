The Spirit of the Archipelago

A living repository of our studio’s evolution, this curated selection brings together structural layout systems, bold typographic studies, and distinct brand identities shaped over years of practice.

Each piece represents a specific moment where discipline met visual intuition—ranging from heavy municipal publication archives and editorial heritage to boundary-pushing modern identity design. Together, these fragments form a continuous visual current, reflecting our commitment to work that is intentional, masterfully structured, and built to endure the drift of time.
